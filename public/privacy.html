This policy explains what personal data Shamba Soko collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and the rights you have under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. Shamba Soko is a farm marketplace at www.shambasoko.store and in the Shamba Soko Android app. It is operated by Modern Talents Hub, a registered business in Kenya ("we", "us"), which is the data controller for Shamba Soko.

This policy covers Shamba Soko only. Other services run by Modern Talents Hub have their own privacy policies.

1. What we collect

When you create an account

  • Your name, phone number, county and whether you are a buyer, seller or rider.
  • Sellers: your business (shop) name.
  • A password, if you sign up with your phone number. Passwords are handled by our login provider (Supabase Auth); we cannot see them.
  • If you use Sign in with Google: your Google account email, name and Google account ID. We do not get your Google password or access to your Gmail, contacts or files.

When you use the marketplace

  • Listings (sellers): product name, category, price, unit, county, photos and an optional video. Photos are re-saved by the app before upload, which removes hidden location (GPS) data from them.
  • Orders (buyers): the items you order, your name, the phone number and delivery address you enter, and the order's progress.
  • WhatsApp number (sellers, optional): if you add one and choose to show it, buyers can contact you on WhatsApp.
  • Reports and disputes: what you report about a listing, photo or order, and any note you add.
  • Riders: vehicle type and details, base location, the areas you cover, whether you are available, and the deliveries you accept.

Verified Seller and Verified Rider (optional)

If you apply for the blue tick, we collect, with your consent: your legal name, phone, ID type and ID number, business name, county, location, the products you sell, four photos taken in the app (face front, face side, your products, and you with your products), and your payment details for the fee (M-Pesa transaction code and payer name). These are private and seen only by Shamba Soko administrators.

Paid promotions (optional)

If you buy a promotion (Featured product, Sponsored advert or Promoted shop), we keep the request, the text you write, the price, the M-Pesa transaction code and payer name, and the promotion dates.

On your device

The app keeps your language choice, your cart and your login session in your browser or phone storage so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not show ads from other companies.

3. Who can see your data

  • Everyone: listings (product, price, county, photos, video) and the seller's shop name; whether a seller is verified; approved promotions.
  • The other person in an order: the seller sees the buyer's name, phone number and delivery address; the buyer sees the seller's name and contact.
  • Signed-in buyers who tap "Contact seller": the seller's phone number, and their WhatsApp number if the seller chose to show it.
  • A rider chosen for a delivery: the pickup county, the delivery address and the items. Once the rider accepts the delivery, the rider, buyer and seller can see each other's names and phone numbers to complete it.
  • Shamba Soko administrators: account details, orders, reports, verification applications and photos, and payment records, only to run and protect the service.
  • Authorities: if Kenyan law requires it, for example a court order.

4. Service providers and where data is stored

We use these providers to run Shamba Soko. They process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, file storage (photos) and logins.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Google: only if you choose Sign in with Google.

These providers' servers may be outside Kenya. Where data is transferred outside Kenya, we do so as allowed by sections 48–50 of the Data Protection Act, 2019. M-Pesa payments are made directly to Safaricom; Shamba Soko never asks for or stores your M-Pesa PIN.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account, listings and photos: until you delete your account or remove them.
  • Orders: kept as a trading record. If you delete your account, your name, phone number and address are removed from your orders.
  • Verification (blue tick): if you are not approved, your ID number and photos are deleted 90 days after the application closes; if you are approved, they are kept while you are verified and for 12 months after it ends; where fraud is suspected, records may be kept for up to 5 years. These rules run automatically. When the period ends we delete the ID number, phone, legal name, location and photos and keep only an outline of the record.
  • Payments for verification and promotions: kept as financial records, even after you delete your account.
  • We may keep data longer while an application is open, during a fraud investigation, or where the law requires it (a legal hold).

6. How we protect your data

  • Connections to Shamba Soko use HTTPS encryption.
  • Database rules limit each person to their own data; only administrators can see verification files, which are kept in private storage.
  • Administrator actions on verification and promotions are recorded in an audit log.

No online service is completely secure. If a personal data breach puts you at risk, we will notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner and you as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used (this policy);
  • see the personal data we hold about you;
  • have wrong or incomplete data corrected;
  • have your data deleted, subject to the record-keeping rules above;
  • object to our use of your data, and withdraw consent at any time (for example, for verification);
  • receive your data in a common format (data portability).

To use these rights, contact us (section 10). We will reply within the time the law allows. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC): www.odpc.go.ke.

8. Delete your account

In the app or website: sign in, open Account (or ☰ → Settings) and tap Delete my account. Your account, profile and listings are deleted straight away, and your name, phone number and address are removed from your orders.

If you cannot sign in: call or WhatsApp 0747 859 353, or email moderntalentshub@gmail.com from the number or email on your account, and ask us to delete it.

Some records are kept as explained in section 5 (verification records within their period, payment records, and anything under a legal hold). If your verification is suspended for investigation, contact support to delete your account.

9. Children

Shamba Soko is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.
